Privacy Policy

OdoTrack

Last updated: 2 July 2026 · Published by SpritzLive

OdoTrack does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing off your device.

No ads No tracking No internet permission No accounts

Information we collect

None. The app collects no personal information and sends nothing to us or anyone else.

  • The app has no internet permission and is technically incapable of sending data anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no analytics or advertising code.

Location

The app requests location permission (GPS) for one reason only: to read your current speed and measure trip distance, and show them on screen. Your location is processed entirely on your device, in real time. It is never sent off the device, and the app keeps no history of where you have been.

Data stored on your device

To work the way you'd expect, the app saves a small amount of information locally on your device only:

  • your trip distance (the odometer figure), so it survives closing and reopening the app, and
  • your unit preference (mph or km/h).

This information stays on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall it. You can reset the trip distance at any time from within the app.

Data sharing

The app shares no data with us or any third party, because it collects no data and has no way to transmit anything.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will be revised.
